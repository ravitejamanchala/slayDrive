1. Practice and safety

Simulator scores, fault assessments and practice routes do not guarantee a test pass or establish that someone is ready to drive on public roads. Use current official guidance and your instructor’s advice. Never operate the simulator while driving a real vehicle or where doing so would distract from a safety-critical activity.
