## Lightweight browser access and animated hero

The hero highlights lightweight, no-install browser practice on desktop and mobile. The feature section explains the existing low-power graphics setting without claiming universal device compatibility.

Two six-second real gameplay loops lead the carousel: Chase view first, then Driver view. Inline H.264 clips are about 598 KB and 510 KB, with no audio. Larger GIFs are available only through explicit download links. Only the selected visible clip loads and plays; offscreen and background-tab video pauses. Pause/Play, previous/next, keyboard browsing and horizontal swipe are available. Reduced-motion and Save-Data users get still images until they explicitly choose Play. Failed playback retains the screenshot.

`scripts/capture-hero-loops.mjs` records actual simulator physics with scripted steering and throttle; `loops-provenance.json` records its source commit. The local browser suite verifies real video playback, automatic advance, inactive/offscreen pause, manual pause, swipe without opening an image, reduced motion and no loop downloads under Save-Data. Browser providers are isolated test fixtures.
