A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before
you go.

A little clarity for the road ahead.

What is SlayDrive?+

SlayDrive is a simulator-style driving practice and learning platform. A £4.99 monthly subscription includes unlimited mock tests, different location maps, Highway Code lessons and road puzzles.

Are the mock tests really unlimited?+

Yes. The £4.99/month membership includes unlimited mock tests. Return to your practice, revisit what you’re learning and make repetition part of your routine.

Does this replace driving lessons or the official test?+

No. SlayDrive is a supplementary learning and practice tool. Simulator practice cannot replace practical instruction, supervised on-road experience or an official driving test. Always use current official Highway Code and DVSA guidance alongside your learning. SlayDrive is not affiliated with or endorsed by the DVSA.

How do different location maps help?+

Different maps give you different settings in which to practise. They encourage you to think about the road situation rather than rely on remembering a familiar route. For details of the map collection, contact info@slaydrive.com.

How do I find out more about membership?+

For membership and platform enquiries, email info@slaydrive.com or call 07741 011350. We can help with questions about the subscription, learning content and tool access.
