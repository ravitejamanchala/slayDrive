Practice between driving lessons

No practice car available?

You don’t need a real car or motor insurance to use an on-screen simulator. If insurance costs or access to a suitable vehicle limit your private practice, you can still work on road awareness at home.

Driving practice without a car

Simulator practice complements driving lessons. It cannot replace supervised on-road experience, provide insurance cover or guarantee a test pass or lower insurance premium. Real-road practice requires the appropriate licence, supervision and insurance.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before
you go.

A little clarity for the road ahead.

How do I choose the best learner driving simulator for me?+

Look for UK road situations, clear controls, useful feedback and a format you will return to. Try a lesson, a mock test and free drive on your own device. SlayDrive also lets you create routes from places you search for. Read our learner simulator guide to decide what fits your practice.

Is SlayDrive good value for money?+

You can try the included courses, mock tests, free drive and My routes at no cost until 1 December 2026. No card required and no automatic subscription. Use that time to see whether it helps your between-lesson practice; it does not replace time with a driving instructor.

Can I use a phone or a controller?+

There are touch controls for mobile, keyboard controls on desktop and support for compatible gamepads. Availability depends on your browser and device. Open Controls for help, and Settings for camera, voice and graphics options.

Is a driving simulator an alternative to driving classes?+

SlayDrive offers additional practice between lessons, especially when extra classes or private practice are difficult to arrange. It helps you explore road decisions, but cannot replace practical driving lessons, supervised road experience or an official test.

Do I need learner driver insurance to use SlayDrive?+

No motor insurance is needed to use an on-screen simulator because you are not driving a real vehicle. SlayDrive does not provide insurance or change the requirements for driving on public roads. Check your cover before any real-road practice.

What is SlayDrive?+

SlayDrive is a browser-based learner driving simulator for practice alongside UK driving lessons. Free access until 1 December 2026 includes courses, road challenges, mock tests, free drive and My routes.

Are the mock tests really unlimited?+

Yes. Try unlimited simulator-style mock tests free until 1 December 2026. No card required. Return to your practice, revisit what you’re learning and make repetition part of your routine.

Does this replace driving lessons or the official test?+

No. SlayDrive is a supplementary learning and practice tool. Simulator practice cannot replace practical instruction, supervised on-road experience or an official driving test. Always use current official Highway Code and DVSA guidance alongside your learning. SlayDrive is not affiliated with or endorsed by the DVSA.

Can I practise roads near me?+

Yes. Open My routes, search for a place and select a start and finish on the map. Generate the route, save it in this browser and drive it in the simulator. Availability depends on map and routing coverage. Road details may be missing or estimated, and no route is an official DVSA test route.

How do I start for free?+

Open the app and sign in or create an account. Try free until 1 December 2026, with no card required. For questions, email info@slaydrive.com. We can help with questions about access, learning content and tool access.
